MYSITE — SIMPLE WEBSITE STARTER

Files:
- index.html: website content
- style.css: layout and design
- script.js: mobile menu and current year

How to preview:
1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in your browser.

How to add Adsterra:
1. Sign up as a publisher at https://adsterra.com/
2. Add your website and follow Adsterra's current verification/approval steps.
3. Create an ad placement and copy the exact code Adsterra provides.
4. Paste it into index.html at one of the comments marked "AD PLACEMENT".
5. Test your site and follow Adsterra's policies. Don't click your own ads or encourage others to click them.

Important:
- The ad areas are placeholders; no ads will show until you add your own approved ad code.
- Replace the sample content, MySite branding, and hello@example.com with your own details.
- Before publishing, add any privacy/cookie disclosures required by your ad provider and applicable law.
